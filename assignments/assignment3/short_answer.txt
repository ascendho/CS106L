Q1. What’s const-correctness and why is it important?
A1. Const-correctness means marking functions and variables that should not modify state as `const`.
	It helps the compiler catch unintended mutations, makes APIs clearer to users, and
	enables reasoning about code safety and optimization.

Q2. Is your class const-correct? How do you know?
A2. Yes — getters such as `getTitle()` and `getPages()` are marked `const`, and helper
	validators that do not modify object state are `const` as well. This guarantees those
	methods won't change the object's fields.
